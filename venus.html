The Hottest Planet

Venus has a thick toxic atmosphere that traps heat, making it the hottest planet in the solar system.

Fun Fact

Venus spins backwards compared to most planets, meaning the Sun rises in the west and sets in the east!

More Venus Facts

  • Hottest planet in the solar system
  • Thick toxic carbon dioxide atmosphere
  • Surface pressure is extremely high
  • Rotates in the opposite direction of most planets
