The Closest Planet to the Sun

Mercury is the smallest planet in the solar system and closest to the Sun. It has no atmosphere to trap heat, causing extreme temperature changes.

Fun Fact

A day on Mercury is longer than its year in some cases!

More Mercury Facts

  • Closest planet to the Sun
  • Has no atmosphere
  • Extreme temperature changes
  • One year = 88 Earth days
