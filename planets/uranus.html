The Ice Giant

Uranus rotates on its side, making it unique among the planets. It has a blue-green colour due to methane gas in its atmosphere and is one of the coldest planets in our solar system.

Fun Fact

Uranus is extremely cold, with temperatures dropping to -224°C, making it the coldest planet in our solar system!

More Uranus Facts

  • An ice giant with a blue-green colour
  • Rotates on its side
  • Has faint rings
