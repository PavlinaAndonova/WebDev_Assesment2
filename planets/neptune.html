The Windiest Planet

Neptune has the strongest winds in the solar system, reaching speeds of up to 2,100 km/h (1,300 mph). These supersonic winds create dynamic storm systems and contribute to Neptune's deep blue colour, which is caused by methane gas in its atmosphere.

Fun Fact

A day on Mercury is longer than its year in some cases!

More Neptune Facts
