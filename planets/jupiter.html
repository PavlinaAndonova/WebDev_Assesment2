The Largest Planet

Jupiter is a gas giant and the largest planet in our solar system. It is known for its colorful bands of clouds and the Great Red Spot, a giant storm that has been raging for centuries.

Fun Fact

Over 1300 Earths could fit inside Jupiter!

More Jupiter Facts

  • Largest planet in the solar system
  • Made mostly of hydrogen and helium
  • Has a giant storm called the Great Red Spot
  • Over 90 known moons
