The Ringed Planet

Saturn is famous for its stunning ring system, made of ice and rock particles. It is a gas giant composed mostly of hydrogen and helium, and is less dense than water. Saturn has many icy moons orbiting it, making it a fascinating world to explore.

Fun Fact

Saturn could float in water because it is less dense than water!
